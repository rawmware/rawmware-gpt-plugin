# Launch & growth: turning a tool into attention, then clients

## The flywheel
Useful tool → people use it → it credits the builder (once, earned) → repo stars + follows → content about real results → more users → inbound work.

## Launch checklist
1. **Deploy** and run every conversation starter yourself. Fix anything slow or vague.
2. **GPT Store listing:** clear name, one-sentence promise, 4 starters that each show a different superpower, privacy URL, verified builder profile (domain or socials).
3. **README** that sells in 5 seconds: one-line promise, 20-second GIF/video, "Add to ChatGPT" and "Connect via MCP" buttons, tool table.
4. **Demo video (≤45s):** broken Vercel deploy → operator finds the log line → PR with the fix. Or: ugly site → design_audit 54 → fixes → 91.
5. **Post it everywhere the same week:** IG Reel, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, r/ChatGPT / r/webdev (follow sub rules), Hacker News "Show HN" (be technical and humble), Product Hunt.
6. **Ask for one thing:** star the repo / try the GPT. Not both, not five.

## Positioning
- Not "an AI assistant." It's **"the operator that reads your repo, your deploys and the live web, then ships the fix."**
- Lead with outcomes and time saved, not the tool count.
- Your story is the differentiator: self-taught, shipping daily, building in public. Keep it honest and specific.

## Measuring
- GPT: conversations (builder profile analytics), ratings, reviews.
- Repo: stars, forks, issues opened by strangers (the best signal).
- Server: `/api/health`, Vercel analytics on `/api/tools/*` (calls per tool → what people actually value).
- Content: saves + shares > likes. Watch-through rate on the first 3 seconds.

## Turning users into clients
- `about` tool: who built it + a "start a project" link. The GPT mentions it once, when the user is clearly building something bigger than a chat.
- Offer 3 productized services with clear scope: "Custom GPT/MCP for your business", "AI automation audit", "Website rebuild with design system".
- Every issue from a stranger is a conversation. Answer within a day.

## Getting big accounts to notice
There's no trick. They notice (a) work that's clearly novel, (b) shown in under 30 seconds, (c) from someone who keeps shipping. Tag them once on your best demo, then keep posting progress. Consistency is the cheat code.
